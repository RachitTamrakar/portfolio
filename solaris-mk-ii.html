Solaris Mk II & Ground Station Equipment Control Electronics

Avionics Vice Lead | Monash High Powered Rocketry | Sep 2023 - Jun 2025

System snapshot

  • Role: Wrote Python control backend; built electrical cabinet; shared ground station ownership and test readiness
  • Environment: Hot-fire testing and IREC 2025 launch operations
  • Interfaces: LabJack DAQs, STM32s, and Raspberry Pis over RS422/CAN/MQTT; long-range pad-control wireless bridge (24+ km)
  • Automation: Automatic fill FSM using capacitive sensor and pressure transducer
  • Status: Operational at IREC 2025; team received the Jim Furfaro Technical Excellence Award

Hardware development

  • Built the main ground station electrical cabinet (DIN rails, wire ducts, ferrules, harnessing).
  • Integrated power distribution, DAQs, networking, relays, motor drivers, wiring.
  • Reworked cabinets and harnessing during tests for reliability and fault isolation.

Custom hardware shipped

  • Problem: Load-cell approach lacked fill accuracy
  • Ownership: Led a 4-person team; owned sensing element, STM32 firmware, calibration
  • Failure + fix: Diagnosed parasitic capacitance; specified actively driven shielded cable
  • Result: Reduced variability to ~10 g; used at IREC 2025 fill; integrated into automatic fill logic

Control & safety

Automated fill sequence (finite state machine):

  • State transitions from capacitive fill and pressure transducer readings
  • Controls fill valve and active vent to hit target mass/temperature
  • Operator initiates; sequence runs autonomously

Safety model:

  • Keyed switch isolates pyro channels while crew are present
  • Fill/dump valves fail to safe on power/control loss
  • Active vent opens on power/control loss
  • Watchdogs on critical links trigger safing on heartbeat loss

Operations:

  • Operated cold-flow and hot-fire tests; debugged electrical/comms faults under time pressure
  • Restored capacitive sensor overnight with propulsion lead
  • Used during IREC 2025 launch operations
