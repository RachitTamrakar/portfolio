Arc-Pyro High Voltage Ignitor

Avionics Vice Lead | Monash High Powered Rocketry | Jan 2025 - Mar 2025

Problem

Develop a reusable, electrically controlled ignition system for a hybrid rocket engine using high-voltage arc pyrolysis instead of traditional pyrotechnic igniters.

My Work

Designed and built a bench-tested 14 kV ignition system for hybrid engine testing.

  • Designed a 14 kV high-voltage power system powered from a 12 V battery
  • Built a portable ignition unit suitable for remote test operation
  • Implemented hardware interlocks and fail-safe mechanisms for operator safety
  • Fabricated and assembled the electrical enclosure and wiring
  • Integrated into ground station electronics for remote operation

Safety Design (High-Voltage)

High-voltage operation required engineering controls:

  • Cabinet limit switches preventing energization when open
  • Manual isolation switches with visible disconnection
  • Electrical arming switch and emergency stop at a safe distance
  • Remote ignition control interface
  • Fail-safe behavior on power or control loss

In addition to hardware controls:

  • Authored formal risk assessments (RA) and safe work instructions (SWI) for high-voltage operation
  • Ran safety inductions for team members prior to system use
  • Supported university safety approval and sign-off for testing

Testing & Results

  • Stable 14 kV arc formation demonstrated during bench testing
  • Successful ABS pyrolysis producing gas for ignition
  • Reliable remote operation with all safety interlocks verified
  • Demonstrated as a proof-of-concept ignition method for future engine tests
