How I Work

I approach engineering with a test-first mindset. I am most comfortable working on systems that must operate correctly in real conditions, not just in simulation. At Bosch, that meant developing vision and automation software used on active manufacturing lines, integrating dozens of cameras across multiple stations with strict cycle-time constraints. Performance issues were visible immediately, so reliability, traceability, and clear failure modes were essential.

In rocketry, it meant building and operating ground-side control and data-acquisition systems during cold-flow and hot-fire testing. I have been responsible for verifying instrumentation, monitoring telemetry, and signing off on test readiness before runs. When problems occurred, I worked through them methodically under time pressure, focusing on collecting enough data to understand the failure before making changes.

I value structured design, clear interfaces, and documentation that reflects how systems are actually used. I am comfortable moving between software, electronics, and test operations depending on what a system needs to function reliably. I learn best by building and validating systems end to end, then leaving them in a state that others can operate, maintain, and extend with confidence.
