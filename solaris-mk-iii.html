Solaris Mk III Support

Supporting assembly and hot-fire testing of 12 kN LOX-Paraffin hybrid rocket engine for Race2Space UK competition

Monash High Powered Rocketry • 2025
LOX Hybrid Propulsion Testing

Project Overview

The Solaris Mk III is our team's most powerful engine to date: a 12 kN LOX-Paraffin hybrid developed for the Race2Space UK propulsion competition. I joined this project in a support capacity to gain exposure to LOX systems and larger-scale propulsion after leading avionics on the smaller Solaris Mk II.

Solaris Mk III hot-fire testing

Engine Specifications

12 kN Thrust

3x more powerful than Solaris Mk II, requiring scaled-up systems and infrastructure.

LOX Oxidizer

Liquid oxygen system presenting unique handling and safety challenges compared to N₂O.

Paraffin Fuel

Solid paraffin fuel grain providing high regression rate for hybrid engine performance.

Race2Space UK

International propulsion competition showcasing cutting-edge student rocketry.

Contributions & Learning

Assembly & Testing Support

Assisted with engine assembly, pre-test setup, and hot-fire operations. Primary value was learning LOX handling procedures, cryogenic safety protocols, and observing how design complexity scales with thrust (Mk III at 12 kN vs Mk II at 4 kN).

Key Takeaways

LOX System Experience

Hands-on with cryogenic oxidizer systems—different material requirements and safety margins vs N₂O.

Scale-Up Understanding

3× thrust increase required fundamental changes in design approach, not just bigger parts.

Test Operations

Participated in high-energy test campaign—reinforced importance of procedural discipline.
