Solaris Mk III Support

Supporting assembly and hot-fire testing of 12 kN LOX-Paraffin hybrid rocket engine for Race2Space UK competition

Monash High Powered Rocketry • 2024-2025
LOX Hybrid Propulsion Testing

Project Overview

The Solaris Mk III represents our team's most powerful engine to date: a 12 kN thrust hybrid rocket engine using liquid oxygen (LOX) and paraffin fuel. This engine was developed for the Race2Space propulsion competition in the UK, representing a significant scale-up from our previous 4 kN Solaris Mk II engine.

I supported the assembly and hot-fire testing campaign, contributing to the team's entry in this international propulsion competition.

Solaris Mk III hot-fire testing

Engine Specifications

12 kN Thrust

3x more powerful than Solaris Mk II, requiring scaled-up systems and infrastructure.

LOX Oxidizer

Liquid oxygen system presenting unique handling and safety challenges compared to N₂O.

Paraffin Fuel

Solid paraffin fuel grain providing high regression rate for hybrid engine performance.

Race2Space UK

International propulsion competition showcasing cutting-edge student rocketry.

My Contributions

Assembly Support

Supported the assembly of the larger-scale engine system, applying lessons learned from Solaris Mk II to the more complex Mk III design. The increased thrust level required more robust components and careful attention to assembly tolerances.

Component Integration

Assisted with integration of engine components, including oxidizer feed system, combustion chamber, nozzle assembly, and instrumentation.

LOX System Assembly

Worked with LOX-compatible materials and components, learning proper handling procedures for cryogenic oxidizer systems.

Instrumentation Setup

Helped install and configure sensors for monitoring engine performance during hot-fire tests.

Hot-Fire Campaign Participation

Participated in the hot-fire testing campaign, supporting test operations and gaining experience with larger-scale propulsion systems.

Test Preparation

Assisted with pre-test setup including LOX loading procedures, sensor verification, and system checks.

Operations Support

Supported test operations from control station, monitoring telemetry and assisting with test procedures.

Post-Test Analysis

Participated in post-test inspections and data review, learning from test results to inform future iterations.

Learning & Experience

LOX System Operations

Gained hands-on experience with liquid oxygen systems, learning critical safety protocols for handling cryogenic oxidizers. LOX presents unique challenges compared to the N₂O systems I worked with on Solaris Mk II, including lower temperatures, higher reactivity, and different material compatibility requirements.

Scale-Up Challenges

Observed firsthand the challenges of scaling up rocket engine systems. The 3x thrust increase from Mk II to Mk III required not just larger components, but fundamental changes in system design, testing infrastructure, and safety margins.

International Competition Experience

Participated in preparations for Race2Space UK competition, experiencing the rigor and professionalism required for international student rocketry competitions.

Skills Developed

Cryogenic Systems

Hands-on experience with LOX handling, storage, and transfer systems.

Large-Scale Testing

Participation in high-thrust engine testing campaign with complex safety protocols.

Team Collaboration

Supporting team members across propulsion, structures, and operations disciplines.

Safety Culture

Learning rigorous safety procedures for high-energy propulsion testing.

Project Context

While my role on Solaris Mk III was supportive rather than leading, this project complemented my deep technical leadership on Solaris Mk II. The experience with a larger-scale engine broadened my understanding of propulsion systems and exposed me to LOX-based hybrid propulsion, expanding beyond the N₂O systems I specialized in.

This project also demonstrated my ability to contribute effectively in different roles—from technical leadership on Mk II to collaborative support on Mk III—adapting to team needs while continuing to learn and grow technically.
